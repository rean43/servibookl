Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a service?

Browse the services, open the one you need, choose an available date and time, and submit your booking request. You can follow its status in My Bookings.

How do I contact a provider?

Open a service or booking and use the Message Provider option when it is available to start a chat.

How does payment work?

For Cash on Service bookings, pay the provider when the service is completed. Confirm other payment details directly with the provider.

How can I offer services?

Create an account and choose the Provider role. After signing in, add your service details from the provider dashboard.
