SERVIBOOK Data Privacy & Terms Notice

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, SERVIBOOK explains below how personal data is used to connect customers with local service providers in San Jose, Occidental Mindoro.

1. Information We Collect

  • Customer accounts: Full name, email address, phone number (if provided), and appointment booking details.
  • Provider accounts: Provider name, contact number (if provided), barangay, street address, map location, operating hours, and service listings.
  • Bookings and messaging: Booking details and status, chat messages, and selected payment method. Payment simulations do not process real payments; card details are not saved.

2. Why We Use This Information

We use the information to create and manage accounts, show provider and service listings, facilitate appointment bookings, let customers and providers communicate, and operate and improve SERVIBOOK.

3. Data Sharing, Security, and Retention

Relevant account, service, and booking information is made available to customers or providers as needed for the platform features they use. SERVIBOOK uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore access controls. Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service and meet applicable obligations; it is not automatically erased when an account stops being active.

4. Your Rights and Requests

You may request access to or correction of your personal data, object to or withdraw consent where applicable, or ask about deletion by contacting SERVIBOOK through the Contact page. Requests may be subject to applicable legal obligations and verification.

Please review this notice before agreeing. Your consent is recorded with your account registration.
